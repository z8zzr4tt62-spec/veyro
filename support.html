Support & Feedback

Welcome to Veyro Support. Find answers to common questions and learn how to get help with the app.

How do I add an IPTV playlist?

Open Veyro, navigate to IPTV and select a supported connection method. Enter your M3U playlist or compatible provider account information.

Does Veyro provide IPTV channels?

No. Veyro is a media player. It does not sell or provide television channels, IPTV subscriptions, movies or playlists. You must use your own authorized content sources.

How do I restore a Premium purchase?

Open the Premium screen in Veyro and select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase.

Why isn't a channel playing?

Check your internet connection, playlist or provider credentials, stream availability and supported media format. Some services may restrict playback or simultaneous connections.

Does Veyro support CarPlay?

CarPlay features depend on your installed Veyro version, vehicle compatibility and supported system capabilities. Video features, where available, are intended only for parked use.

How do I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Open Help & Feedback inside Veyro on your iPhone. You can suggest new features, report problems or share ideas to improve the experience.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open your Apple Account subscription settings on your iPhone. You can also visit Apple Billing Support.

How do I request a refund?

For purchases processed by Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact Support

For customer support, technical assistance, feature suggestions, or bug reports, contact us at veyro.support@icloud.com. You can also use Help & Feedback inside the Veyro iPhone app.

Please include your app version, the problem and steps to reproduce it. Never send passwords, payment-card details or IPTV account credentials.
