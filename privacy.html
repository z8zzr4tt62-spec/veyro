Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Veyro is a media player for compatible, user-provided streaming sources. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the Veyro application.

1. Information You Provide

You may enter IPTV playlists, service addresses, account usernames, passwords and other settings. Veyro uses this information to connect to services you configure. Relevant settings and credentials may be stored on your device, including through platform-protected storage.

2. IPTV Providers

When you connect to a third-party streaming provider, that provider may receive your connection details, credentials, IP address and playback requests. Those services operate under their own privacy practices. Some providers use unencrypted HTTP connections, which may expose transmitted information.

3. Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases and subscriptions. Veyro uses transaction and entitlement information supplied through Apple's StoreKit system to determine Premium access. Veyro does not receive your complete payment-card details.

4. Free Preview

Veyro may store a record of your cumulative free-preview usage on your device to manage your remaining allowance. The current implementation does not use a production server-side trial ledger.

5. Feedback and Support

If you choose to contact support, information you provide may include your email address, message and optional app version details. Feedback is sent through your chosen email or sharing application. We use submitted information to respond to requests and improve Veyro.

6. Data Sharing

Veyro does not sell personal information to advertisers. Information may be processed by Apple, by streaming providers you choose to connect to, or by services you select when contacting support.

7. Storage and Security

Veyro uses available platform security protections for locally stored information. However, no application or internet connection can guarantee absolute security. Device backups and third-party services may have separate data-handling practices.

8. Retention and Deletion

Locally stored settings may be removed through available application controls or by deleting the application, subject to device backup behavior. Apple manages purchase records under its own policies. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to requests. You may contact us to request deletion of support information we hold, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Veyro is not specifically designed for children. Users are responsible for selecting suitable streaming sources and content.

10. Content and Rights

Veyro does not provide television channels, movies, IPTV subscriptions or playlists. Users are responsible for accessing content they are authorized to use.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as Veyro changes. The effective date will reflect material revisions.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, visit our Support page. A public support email will be added before launch.
